MLK Day Weekend in Boulder: January 16 to 18, 2027

Martin Luther King Jr. Day falls on Monday, January 18, 2027, which makes a three-day weekend in Boulder. Here is what we can verify for a group planning it: a CU men's basketball home game on Saturday evening, a county office closure on Monday, and the Sundance Film Festival opening the Thursday after the holiday.

The Weekend at a Glance

  • Saturday, January 16, 2027, 6 pm: the Buffs men's basketball team hosts Houston at the CU Events Center. See the home schedule.
  • Monday, January 18, 2027, 8 am to 5 pm: Boulder County government offices are closed in observance of Martin Luther King Jr. Day, according to the county's own listing.
  • Thursday, January 21, 2027: the Sundance Film Festival opens in Boulder and runs through Sunday, January 31. Read our Sundance pages.

A Note on Sundance

The festival does not overlap the holiday weekend. It opens three days after Martin Luther King Jr. Day, on Thursday, January 21, so a group can make the holiday weekend a quiet lead-in to the first Boulder festival. The Sundance organizer says single-film tickets go on sale January 8, 2027, at 10 am MT.

What We Have Not Found Yet

We have not found a dated public event for the weekend beyond the game and the county closure. We will add any event the organizer posts, and we will not list one until we can check it on the organizer's own page.
