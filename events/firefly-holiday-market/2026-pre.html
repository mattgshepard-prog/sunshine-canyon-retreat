Firefly Handmade Holiday Market: Two Days of Colorado Makers on Pearl Street

On the weekend of November 14 and 15, 2026, the Pearl Street Mall fills with tents, and inside them some of the best makers in the region: potters, jewelers, woodworkers, printmakers, textile artists. The Firefly Handmade Holiday Market is the handmade counterweight to big-box holiday shopping, and it happens right in the middle of downtown Boulder, free to walk through.

This page carries the facts as the organizer has posted them; check the Firefly site as the weekend approaches for the vendor list and any updates.

The Facts As They Stand

  • Dates: Saturday, November 14 and Sunday, November 15, 2026.
  • Where: Outdoors on the Pearl Street Pedestrian Mall, downtown Boulder.
  • Hours: 10 am to 4 pm, Saturday and Sunday.
  • Cost: Free to attend.
  • What it is: A seasonally curated, artisan-led market. Firefly runs markets in Boulder and Denver; the Boulder holiday edition is the one on Pearl Street.
  • Official page: fireflyhandmade.com/markets/holiday-market-boulder

All market facts from the Firefly Handmade official market page, checked October 5, 2026. The organizer has since posted hours (10 am to 4 pm both days) and a feature list including 80+ artists; the individual artisan lineup is on the organizer's page.

Why We Like This One for a Group Weekend

This is a market you can build a whole Saturday around without planning a minute of it. Wander the stalls with a coffee, let everyone find their own gifts, regroup for lunch on Pearl, wander again. Mid-November in Boulder sits at the tail of the gold: the cottonwoods along the creek are often still holding their last leaves that weekend, and the light in the canyon in the late afternoon is worth the drive up on its own.

For a group of twelve, the house works the same way it does for a wedding weekend or a game day: everyone sleeps under one roof, the kitchen holds a crowd, and downtown is fifteen minutes down the canyon when you are ready for it. After a day among the crowds, the quiet of Sunshine Canyon at dusk is its own event.

Practical notes: the market is outdoors, so dress for whatever November gives you, anything from shirtsleeves to flurries. The walking is easy once you are downtown; the mall is four car-free blocks with the stalls along them. One thing to leave at the house: the organizer notes that pets are not permitted on the Pearl Street Mall per the City of Boulder, so this is a people-only wander. For driving in, the organizer recommends the City of Boulder parking garages, where parking is free all day Saturday and Sunday: boulderdowntown.com/visit/parking/parking-garages.

If You Are Coming for the Market

The weekend of November 14 and 15 is early enough in the season that Boulder's beds are still gettable, but holiday weekends train guests to book early. If the plan is crafts on Saturday and a slow Sunday in the canyon, lock the house in now and let the rest of the weekend take care of itself.
