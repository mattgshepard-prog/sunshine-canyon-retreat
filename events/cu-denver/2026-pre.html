A Weeknight in the Events Center

A Tuesday tip gives the afternoon back to the group. Take the time to walk the downtown blocks, find a table before the dinner rush, and let the day wind down at its own pace. Winter in Boulder means the light is low and gold by late afternoon, so a warm layer belongs in the bag before anyone heads for the arena.

Plan the Evening

Settle dinner before tip so no one is hungry through the first half. Decide on the ride back before anyone leaves the building: for a group, that means one driver who has not had a drink, or a car set up in advance. The game ends with the canyon still ahead, so a quiet nightcap at home is the easy finish.
