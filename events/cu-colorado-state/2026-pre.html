CU vs Colorado State: Men's Basketball, Saturday, December 5, 2026

The Buffs men's basketball team hosts Colorado State at the CU Events Center on Saturday, December 5, 2026, with tip at 8 pm. It is one of the early-December home dates on the schedule, and it falls on the same weekend as the CU Presents Holiday Festival at Macky Auditorium, so a group can plan an afternoon of holiday music and an evening of college basketball in one Boulder day.

The Facts

  • Date: Saturday, December 5, 2026
  • Tip: 8 pm
  • Venue: CU Events Center, Boulder (a home game)
  • Opponent: Colorado State

A Saturday in Boulder

The same weekend, CU Presents stages the Holiday Festival at Macky Auditorium, with performances at 1 pm and 4 pm on Saturday, December 5. A group that wants a daytime concert and an evening game can plan both. The festival's times, the venue, and what to know before you go are on our Holiday Festival page.

Plan the Evening

December nights in Boulder are cold once the sun drops behind the Flatirons. Bring a warm layer for the walk, plan dinner before tip, and settle on how the group gets back up the canyon afterward. A game night is easier when the plan is set before anyone leaves the house.
