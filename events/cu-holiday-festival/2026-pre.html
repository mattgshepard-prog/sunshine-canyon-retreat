CU Presents Holiday Festival at Macky: December 4 to 6, 2026

The CU Boulder College of Music's Holiday Festival fills Macky Auditorium for three days in early December: student ensembles, standout soloists, and faculty musicians in one holiday program. For a group that wants one indoor evening every age in the room can share, it is the place to start.

The Performances

  • Friday, December 4, 2026, 7:30 pm
  • Saturday, December 5, 2026, 1 pm and 4 pm
  • Sunday, December 6, 2026, 4 pm

Every performance is at Macky Auditorium, 1595 Pleasant Street, on the CU Boulder campus. Tickets are sold through CU Presents, and each performance is sold as its own show.

Dates and times from CU Presents, cupresents.org/show-details/holiday-festival-2026, checked November 23, 2026. Street address from the CU Boulder events calendar listing, calendar.colorado.edu/event/holiday-festival-2026.

What to Expect

The festival is a College of Music production. CU Presents says CU Director of Bands Shanti Simon takes the helm as director of the event this year, and the program draws on the orchestras, bands, choirs, and faculty of the College of Music. The stage is dressed in twinkling lights and festive greenery, and the music runs from beloved classics to a few new pieces.

One note from the organizer: this production uses theatrical haze effects. If anyone in your group is sensitive to haze, call CU Presents before the show.

Tickets and Questions

Single tickets are on sale now through CU Presents at cupresents.org. The box office can be reached at 303.492.8008, Monday to Friday, 10 am to 5 pm, or at cupresents@colorado.edu. Each performance has its own ticket price, so check the link for the show you want.

Box office hours and contact details from cupresents.org, checked November 23, 2026.

Planning a Group Weekend

Friday night is the first chance to fill the room. Saturday offers two performances, an afternoon and a late afternoon, so a group can pick the time that fits its dinner plans. Sunday's 4 pm show closes the run, which makes a good last evening before the week starts. On Saturday, December 5, the Buffs men's basketball team also hosts Colorado State, at 8 pm at the CU Events Center, so a group that wants both has a full Boulder Saturday.
