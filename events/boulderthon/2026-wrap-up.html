Boulderthon 2026: The Morning After

Race day came and went in a blur of chalk-marked roads, cowbells on Pearl Street, and one very long exhale at the finish line. More than seventeen thousand runners came up the canyon road to Boulder for the marathon, half, 10K and 5K. Here is where everything landed, and where next year is already waiting.

Monday, September 28. Everything below comes from the official results site and boulderthon.org, checked today.

The Overall Winners

Four races, four mornings-of-glory stories. The chip times live on the official results site, so we name only the winners here and send you straight to the source for every other finisher, age group and split.

  • Marathon: Finnian Jacobson-Schulte, 2:30:18; Sasha Neglia, first woman, 2:58:39
  • Half marathon: Reed Fischer, 1:04:31; Emily Durgin, first woman, 1:16:40
  • 10K: Dylan Van Der Hock, 31:35; Hannah Reinhardt, first woman, 36:05
  • 5K: Morgan Beadlescomb, 13:47; Nigsti Molla Hagos, first woman, 15:26

Full results for all four distances: results.laurelt.com/bld/results. Results posted by the race timer; we link rather than reproduce, so what you see there is always the current official version.

Find Your Time, Your Photos, Your People

If you ran, the results page has your chip time, your pace, and where you stood in your age group. Search by name or bib number, filter by distance, and take a minute with it. Six months of training earns at least that.

Race photos are on the same site. If your photos did not attach to your bib automatically, the photo claim page matches lost shots to your race record: results.laurelt.com/bld/photos/claim.

Watching someone else run it? The tracking link the race used on Sunday is still live for replay: track.laurelt.com.

2027: Sunday, September 26

Registration for 2027 is already open on the race site, and the date is set: Sunday, September 26, 2027. Same foothills, same finish line energy, same Pearl Street expo. If this year was your first taste of a Boulder race weekend and you are already plotting the next one, the organizer's page has the date and the registration link: boulderthon.org.
