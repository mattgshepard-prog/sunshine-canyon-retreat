CU Boulder Winter Recognition Ceremonies: December 2026

CU Boulder holds no campuswide ceremony in December. Its colleges, schools, and programs hold their own winter recognition ceremonies and receptions for fall graduates, from Friday, December 4 to Saturday, December 12, 2026. Below is the schedule by date, from the university's own page.

Friday, December 4

  • College of Communication, Media, Design and Information reception: 10:30 am to 12:30 pm, Center for Academic Success and Engagement, room E330 Multipurpose Room.

Friday, December 11

  • Army ROTC commissioning: 4 pm to 6 pm, Center for Academic Success and Engagement Auditorium, reception to follow.
  • Leeds School of Business reception: 4 pm to 5 pm, Koelbel Building, Olson Atrium (reception only).
  • Arts and Sciences Honors Program convocation: 5 pm to 8 pm, University Memorial Center, Glenn Miller Ballroom, reception to follow.
  • Astrophysical and Planetary Sciences: 5 pm to 7 pm, Fiske Planetarium and Science Center, reception in the Fiske lobby.
  • Geography: 5 pm to 7 pm, Guggenheim Building room 205, reception in the Guggenheim lobby from 6 pm to 7 pm.
  • Physics and Engineering Physics: 5 pm to 7 pm, Duane Physics room G1B20, reception in the Duane lobby.

Saturday, December 12

  • Naval ROTC commissioning: 9 am to 12 pm, Center for Academic Success and Engagement Auditorium.
  • Arts and Sciences, Division of Social Sciences: 9 am to 12 pm, University Memorial Center, Glenn Miller Ballroom, reception to follow.
  • Molecular, Cellular and Developmental Biology: 9 am to 12 pm, Gold Biosciences room A2B70, reception in the same room.
  • Aerospace Engineering Sciences: 9 am to 12 pm, Macky Auditorium; reception 12:30 pm to 2:30 pm in the Aerospace Engineering Sciences Building lobby.
  • Electrical, Computer and Energy Engineering: 9 am to 12 pm, Macky Auditorium (doors open 9 am, ceremony at 10 am).
  • Computer Science: 9 am to 12 pm, the ceremony listing gives Macky Auditorium and the Engineering Center, room ECCS 114.
  • Ecology and Evolutionary Biology: 1 pm to 4 pm, Center for Academic Success and Engagement Auditorium, reception in the Chancellor's Hall.
  • College of Engineering and Applied Science (all Engineering and Computer Science degrees): 10 am to 12 pm, Macky Auditorium (doors open 9 am).
  • Mechanical Engineering: 10 am to 12 pm, Macky Auditorium, reception in the Engineering Lobby.

All times, rooms, and receptions from the CU Boulder Winter Recognition Ceremonies page, colorado.edu/commencement/ceremonies-events/winter-conferral/winter-recognition-ceremonies, checked November 23, 2026. Some units list their rooms differently in the full page, so check the unit's contact on that page before you go.

Units Without a Winter Ceremony

Several units hold no winter recognition ceremony, among them the College of Music, the School of Law, and the Department of Linguistics. Those graduates are invited to take part in the spring commencement instead. The full list is at the end of the university's page.

Parking and Planning

The university posts a separate parking page for winter recognition ceremonies, at colorado.edu/commencement/node/198. A Macky Auditorium ceremony at 9 am or 10 am on December 12 is an early start for a family, so plan the drive and parking the evening before.
