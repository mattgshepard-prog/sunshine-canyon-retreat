Planning a Home-Game Weekend

Most home dates fall on a weeknight or a Saturday, and the Saturday tips range from one in the afternoon to eight in the evening. An afternoon tip leaves time for a long dinner in town afterward. An evening tip is a good reason to settle the group for the night before the game, rather than driving back up the canyon in the dark.

Two home weekends in the new year stand out for groups. The Saturday, January 9 game against West Virginia and the Saturday, February 13 game against Baylor each give a group a full weekend around a midday tip.
