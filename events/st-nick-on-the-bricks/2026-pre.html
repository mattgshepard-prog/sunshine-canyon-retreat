St. Nick on the Bricks: Santa on Pearl Street, Four Dates Deep Into the Season

The day after Thanksgiving, and on three Saturdays after it, Santa sets up shop indoors at the Downtown Boulder Visitor Information Center at 1303 Pearl Street. The visits are free, the hours are unhurried, and unlike the mall line you remember from childhood, this one comes with a walk on the Pearl Street Mall on either side of it. Friday November 27 and Saturdays November 28, December 5 and December 12, 2026, 11 am to 2 pm each date.

Every fact below comes from the organizer's own page, checked October 29, 2026. We will keep this page current as the dates approach.

The Facts As They Stand

  • Dates and time: Friday, November 27 and Saturdays, November 28, December 5 and December 12, 2026, 11 am to 2 pm on each date.
  • Where: The Downtown Boulder Visitor Information Center, 1303 Pearl Street, Boulder. Indoors, which matters more than it sounds in a Colorado December.
  • Cost: Visits with Santa are free. The organizer asks for a suggested donation per group; the current amount is posted on the organizer's page.
  • What to bring: The organizer's own words are the best advice there is: bring your list and a camera. There is no professional photo operation to wait behind, so the photos you leave with are the ones you take yourself.
  • Downtown Dash: If your group is doing the Downtown Dash, ask the Visitor Services staff for a stamp for your visit with Santa.
  • Who runs it: Downtown Boulder, part of its Light Up the Holidays season of events downtown from late November into December.
  • Official page: boulderdowntown.com/light-up-the-holidays/events/st-nick-on-the-bricks

Where These Dates Sit in the Boulder Season

The Friday date lands on the busiest family weekend of the Boulder calendar. Thanksgiving itself belongs to the Thanksgiving Day 5K in front of the courthouse Thursday morning, and the Nutcracker plays Macky Auditorium across the weekend. A Santa visit slots neatly between a Friday matinee of Boulder Ballet's The Nutcracker and dinner downtown.

The Saturday dates are quieter and, honestly, easier. December 5 is COLDERBolder morning on the CU campus, close enough that a 9 am wave and an 11 am Santa line fit in one morning if your crew is ambitious. December 12 pairs Santa from 11 to 2 with the Lights of December Parade at 6 pm, which makes it the single best build-a-day-around-it date of the four: Santa after lunch, a late hot chocolate, then curbside seats for floats and fire engines rolling up the middle of town after dark.

Planning a Group Visit

If you are the one holding the calendar for a crew of kids and their adults, the working plan is simple. Aim for the opening hour: the line is shortest before the lunch crowd arrives, and the light on Pearl Street is at its low winter gold around then. The Center is at 1303 Pearl, on the east end of the pedestrian mall, so the walk in from any downtown parking structure doubles as the event. Send one adult ahead to hold the spot and let everyone else wander the blocks around it.

The camera instruction deserves a repeat: the photos are yours to take. That is the gift of this format, a five-year-old gets a whole unhurried conversation about the list instead of a conveyor-belt lap photo, and the picture you take of it will be better anyway.

If your group is staying with us, the house sleeps up to fourteen in five bedrooms, about fifteen minutes up the canyon from Pearl Street. The shape of the day writes itself: everyone down the hill for late morning, Santa and the list and the walk on the bricks, then the drive back up Sunshine Canyon Drive while the kids compare notes in the back seats, and the hot tub in the cold afternoon light for whoever wants it.

If You Are Planning Around Santa

These are the dates the season anchors itself to, and the weekends of December 5 and December 12 are when out-of-town groups are deciding. The earlier the house is locked in, the more of the season you get to keep.
