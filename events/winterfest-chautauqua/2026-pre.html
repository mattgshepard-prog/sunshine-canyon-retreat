Winterfest at Colorado Chautauqua: December 10 to 12, 2026

Chautauqua's Winterfest runs three days this year, starting Thursday, December 10, with the tree lighting on Friday evening and the full festival on Saturday, December 12. The historic buildings and evergreens at 900 Baseline Road take on the season, and the free parts of the weekend are plenty for a group that wants a winter afternoon without a ticket line.

The Schedule

  • Thursday, December 10, 7:30 pm: LAPOMPE's Very Django Holiday Show, in the Community House (ticketed)
  • Friday, December 11, 4 pm: Jeff & Paige's Rainbow Socks Holiday Kids' Concert, in the Community House (ticketed), followed by the tree lighting and a lantern walk to the Fairy Forest
  • Friday, December 11, 5:15 pm: the tree lighting, free admission
  • Saturday, December 12, 10 am to 4 pm: the festival day, with Santa's Cottage, the Fairy Forest, local shopping, cookie decorating, craft making, horse-drawn carriage rides, historic cottage tours, and Breakfast with Santa

Schedule from the Colorado Chautauqua event page, chautauqua.com/event/winterfest-2026/, checked November 23, 2026.

What Is Free and What Needs a Ticket

The organizer says no pass is required this year. Tickets are needed for the two holiday concerts, Breakfast with Santa, the carriage rides, and the historic cottage tours. Santa's Cottage is free, with printed portraits for sale. Craft making and cookie decorating are paid at the door. The organizer says all other activities are free.

From the same Chautauqua page.

Getting There

Winterfest is at Colorado Chautauqua, 900 Baseline Road, Boulder, CO 80302, on the grounds below the Flatirons.
