CU vs West Virginia: Men's Basketball, Saturday, January 9, 2027

The Buffs host West Virginia at the CU Events Center on Saturday, January 9, 2027, with tip at 1 pm. It is a midday tip in the middle of January, the kind of afternoon that suits a group that wants a basketball game and an unhurried evening back in the canyon.

The Facts

  • Date: Saturday, January 9, 2027
  • Tip: 1 pm
  • Venue: CU Events Center, Boulder (a home game)
  • Opponent: West Virginia

Planning the Weekend

A one o'clock tip leaves the evening free. A group can have a late breakfast in town, watch the game, and be back in the canyon for dinner. In January the days are short, so plan the drive home for daylight if the group is driving down for the afternoon.
