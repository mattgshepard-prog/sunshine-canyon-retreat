Tebo Train's Snowflake Express: Sunday Rides on Pearl Street

The 100% electric Tebo Train, riding this season as the Snowflake Express, runs along the 1200 block of Pearl Street on three Sundays: November 29, December 6, and December 13, 2026. It is a short, slow ride for the youngest people in a group, and a good reason to walk the downtown blocks before lunch.

The Dates and Times

  • Sunday, November 29, 2026, 11 am to 2 pm
  • Sunday, December 6, 2026, 11 am to 2 pm
  • Sunday, December 13, 2026, 11 am to 2 pm

The last train leaves the station at 2 pm. The Snowflake Express boards in front of Wells Fargo at 1242 Pearl Street.

From the organizer's event page, boulderdowntown.com/do/tebo-trains-snowflake-express, checked November 23, 2026.

Who Rides

Children ages 10 and under ride, with their accompanying adults. For a family group, that means the grown-ups ride too.

If the Weather Turns

The organizer says the Snowflake Express may be canceled in inclement weather. On a snowy Sunday, check the organizer's page before you leave the canyon.

Downtown Dash

If your group is taking part in the Downtown Dash this year, the organizer says to stop by the Visitor Information Center after your train ride and show your train sticker to earn a stamp.

Downtown Dash detail from the same organizer page.
