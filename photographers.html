The invitation

You have the dates picked.
Now you need somewhere to put them.

Fall sessions in Boulder County usually mean open space with a permit, or a spot you have used three years running and are a little tired of. Crowds on a Saturday. Somebody else's family wandering through your background.

This is a house I own at 6186 Sunshine Canyon Drive. It is a short-term rental most of the year, and it is available to photographers by the session on the evenings nobody has booked it.

You get the property to yourself. The cleared bench around the house, three decks, the tree line below, and indoor space if the weather turns. Shoot what you want and keep everything you make. There is no shot list and nobody standing over you.

The house normally goes out on a three-night minimum, which is not what you need. That is exactly why this is a separate conversation.

What you get

  • Frames are yours, no release required
  • Three decks, one covered and heated
  • Indoor space, a real bathroom, somewhere to change
  • Six to eight parking spots on the turnaround
  • Weather moves you under cover, not to another date

Practical

What you are actually working with

The straight version, including the parts that are not flattering. You are going to find these out anyway, and better now than with eight families in the car behind you.

Getting there
About 15 minutes up Sunshine Canyon Drive from the end of Mapleton. The last mile is gravel. Any normal car handles it dry. Tell your clients so nobody is surprised.
Parking
Six to eight vehicles on a gravel turnaround. Enough for back-to-back sessions if families overlap by a few minutes.
The trees
Ponderosa pine, wall to wall. There are deciduous pockets right by the house that turn in late September. This is not an aspen grove and I am not going to pretend it is.
The ground
A cleared dirt-and-dry-grass bench around the house, pine forest falling away below. No lawn and no golden meadow. The decks and the tree line are what you work with.
Three decks
An open deck with a louvered pergola and seating for eight, a covered deck with a solid roof and an overhead heater, and a small upper balcony.
Weather
The covered deck is heated and there is indoor space. A September cell or an October cold snap moves the session, not the date. Somewhere warm for the next family to wait.
Permits
None. Private property, so no Boulder County open space permit and no ranger conversation.
Privacy
The tree line screens well at ground level, but there are neighbors on the hillside. You will not see them in your frames. You will see them from the driveway.
The basics
Real bathroom, power, and a house to change in. Not a trailhead.

Three nights, or one evening

As a rental the house has a three-night minimum, so nobody can book a single night out from under you. A session fee is the only way to get one evening here, and it is a fraction of a three-night stay.
